Background & Philosophy

As a 3rd-year computer science student at the University of Bahrain, my work sits at the intersection of raw computational logic, structural mathematics, physical systems, and expressive design. Rejecting corporate minimalism for stark, high-contrast brutalist principles.

Operational Workflow

  1. Conceptualize core architecture and logical constraints.
  2. Deconstruct complex systems into foundational mathematics and physics components.
  3. Execute visual or programmatic solutions under high-contrast aesthetic frameworks.
  4. Refine through iterative deployment, testing, and Git revision controls.
